# TLD Technical Standard v3.0 — Geometry Extension

Status: v0.3.0 method-freeze candidate. TLD v2.1 remains the historical one-dimensional standard.

## Semantic boundary

TLD is a structural assay, not proof of a physical theory. `T_e`, `S_e`, `winner_N`, operation depth `r`, and geometric scale `ell` are distinct:

- `T_e` is available only when a registered operator is recursively applied over meaningful operation depth.
- `S_e` is persistence over nonredundant meaningful depth and is otherwise `NOT_APPLICABLE`.
- `winner_N` is a closure-mode label compared with parent-matched null closure distributions.
- `ell` is geometric scale and must never be renamed `T_e`.
- harmonic or closure index `N` must never be backfilled as emergence time.

## Required order

1. establish source custody and a valid geometry pack;
2. freeze parents, nested replicates, projections, nulls, perturbations, operators, depths, scales, channels, baseline, and claim ceiling;
3. pass GeometryScoutV1;
4. calculate registered channels without outcome-dependent substitution;
5. independently recompute endpoints without importing production adjudication functions;
6. adjudicate under the frozen method and package all failures.

## Candidate family

The v3 calibration considers exactly three methods.

### Method A — strict conjunctive binary gate

Requires Scout eligibility, calibrated bidirectional separation, adequate nulls, canonical TLD construct recovery, structured fragility, two faithful agreeing projections, and exclusion by no stronger baseline.

### Method B — hierarchical binary gate

Requires Scout eligibility, adequate nulls, primary bidirectional separation, representation agreement, and baseline exclusion, followed by at least one calibrated support channel: closure-null calibration, structured fragility, or a geometry/topology invariant.

### Method C — nonbinary evidence vector

Reports every calibrated channel separately. It performs no uncalibrated numerical pooling and cannot mint a binary TLD-positive result. Method C is the legal freeze when the evidence channels are useful but binary sensitivity, specificity, or semantic mapping is inadequate.

The chosen method is determined only by the frozen loss over synthetic fixtures and historical controls.

## Required channels

Signed bidirectional separation reports observed-minus-null effect, robust standardized effect, upper/lower/two-sided probabilities, direction, parent and nested-parent uncertainty, and multiplicity control. “Greater than null” is not forced as the only structural direction.

Closure-null calibration reports `winner_N`, the null-winner distribution, local closure probability, effect size, boundary rate, interior relative-curvature diagnostics, and mode stability. A flat or all-tied trace is invalid for elbow selection. Centered curvature excludes endpoints.

Canonical construct recovery distinguishes the published TLD I closed, escaped, and healed sequences from registered order and value controls without replacing the historical sequence operator with a geometry proxy.

Structured fragility compares an observed perturbation response with a preregistered fingerprint. A valid structure need not survive arbitrary destruction.

Representation agreement compares at least two preregistered faithful projections. Projection disagreement is reported, not averaged away.

Domain-baseline exclusion asks whether a simpler, scientifically appropriate baseline explains the same endpoint at least as well.

## Null and perturbation requirements

Every null child belongs to exactly one parent. Null families state the target destroyed and nuisances preserved, retain geometry where required, use frozen seeds, and undergo variance, tie, boundary, and direction-bias audits. Null-family selection after outcomes invalidates the assay.

Perturbations must be independently meaningful and nonredundant with nulls. Their strengths and expected fragility shapes are registered before outcomes.

## Measurement policy

Deterministic semantic assertions use an authorized clean run and, when claim-bearing, a duplicate clean replay. Noisy numeric observables use preregistered repetitions and variance-floor analysis. Timing/resource observables require warmup and randomized order and remain non-authoritative for causal ownership.

## Claim ceiling

The geometry method may reach `CALIBRATED_ASSAY` only after calibration and a protocol-valid held-out run. `TLD_DERIVED` remains separately adjudicated. TLD results do not authorize source ownership, patches, repair counts, deployment, self-maintenance, or theory proof.
