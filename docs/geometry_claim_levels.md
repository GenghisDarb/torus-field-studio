# Geometry Claim Levels

Geometry-indexed outputs use bounded claim levels.

## `DESCRIPTIVE`

Source custody, geometry, projections, raw summaries, and uncalibrated channel values are described. No construct or field classification is implied.

## `COMPUTED_DYNAMICAL`

Registered operators and endpoints were computed and independently checked. This supports a reproducible calculation, not construct validity or external physical validation.

## `CALIBRATED_ASSAY`

The frozen method passed its synthetic and historical calibration boundary and was executed on a protocol-valid held-out field. The exact positive, negative, mixed, blocked, invalidated, or nonbinary evidence-vector outcome must be stated.

## `EXTERNAL_VALIDATION`

Reserved for independent, appropriately powered replication beyond the source, authors, calibration, and dataset-selection lineage. TORUS Field Studio v0.3.0 does not grant this level.

`TLD_DERIVED` is adjudicated separately from the scientific outcome. A closure label is not `T_e`; a geometric scale is not `T_e`; `S_e` is not inferred from redundant depth; and a negative registered projection does not prove absence of all structure.

Forbidden claims include theory proof, a physical 14-fold law from TORUS-BROT, self-maintaining software, ControllerGate production readiness, source ownership, patch or repair authorization, and generalized AMDS effectiveness. Metrology exports are shadow evidence only.

The executable support wording is: structure receives support when it separates from a matched null that destroys the target relation while preserving registered nuisances, exhibits a preregistered fragility fingerprint, remains consistent across faithful representations, and is not explained by a stronger domain baseline. Appropriate independently meaningful perturbations may add support; arbitrary destructive survival is not required.
