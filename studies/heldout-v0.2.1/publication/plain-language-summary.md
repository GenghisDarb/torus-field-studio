The separately registered closure objective selected `winner_N = 9`. It must not be relabeled as `T_e` or physical time. N=14 failed the secondary specificity gate.

All 12 station parents were eligible, the execution failure ledger was empty, the independent verifier reported zero disagreements, and all 25 mutation controls were rejected. The correct claim level is `COMPUTED_DYNAMICAL`; `TLD_DERIVED` is blocked and `EXTERNALLY_VALIDATED` is false.
